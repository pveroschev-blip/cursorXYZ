Privacy Policy — Cursor XYZ

Add-in for Autodesk Fusion · Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Pavel Eroshev, Germany
E-mail: p.v.eroschev@gmail.com

2. Summary

Cursor XYZ does not collect, store or transmit your designs, models, cursor positions, usage statistics or any other personal data to the publisher. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising in the add-in.

3. License check

To confirm that you purchased the add-in, Cursor XYZ sends one request to the Autodesk App Store (apps.autodesk.com). The request contains only:

  • your Autodesk user ID (an anonymous identifier provided by Fusion, not your name or e-mail), and
  • the App Store ID of Cursor XYZ.

Autodesk answers only whether the purchase is valid. The request goes directly from your computer to Autodesk; the publisher does not receive or see it. The check runs when the Cursor XYZ palette is opened and is repeated at most once every 14 days.

Processing by Autodesk is governed by the Autodesk Privacy Statement. Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Data stored on your computer

After a successful check, the add-in saves a small file %APPDATA%\CursorXYZ\license.json containing your Autodesk user ID and the date until which the check is valid. This lets the add-in work offline for 14 days. The file never leaves your computer.

5. Retention and deletion

The publisher stores no data about you. To delete the local file, uninstall the add-in or delete the folder %APPDATA%\CursorXYZ. Data related to your purchase is held by Autodesk and PayPal under their own privacy policies.

6. Your rights and withdrawing consent

You can stop all data transfer at any time by uninstalling the add-in. Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For questions, contact the e-mail address above.

7. Third parties

The only third party involved is Autodesk (license check, sales) and, for payments, PayPal. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
